# 📊 Super Store Sales Analysis & Forecasting Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Analysis and Forecasting Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective of this project is to analyze Super Store sales data and identify important business trends related to sales, profit, customers, products, regions, shipping modes, and order performance.

The project also includes a **Sales Forecasting Dashboard** to analyze historical sales trends and estimate future sales performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the most profitable and high-performing categories
- Analyze sales across different regions and states
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Analyze sales based on shipping modes
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends
- Compare sales and profit performance over time
- Identify top-performing states and categories
- Create an interactive sales forecasting dashboard
- Provide meaningful business insights using data visualization

# 💡 Key Business Insights

Some important insights identified through the analysis include:

### 1. Regional Performance

The **West region** contributes a significant share of total sales compared with other regions.

### 2. Customer Segment

The **Consumer segment** is one of the major contributors to overall sales.

### 3. Product Categories

Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies are the major product categories analyzed in the dashboard.

### 4. Shipping Performance

**Standard Class** is the most commonly used shipping mode and contributes a significant portion of total sales.

### 5. Monthly Trends

Sales performance varies across months, with noticeable increases during certain periods of the year.

### 6. State Performance

The dashboard identifies the top-performing states based on total sales, helping understand geographical sales concentration.
